Diamond Casino

No cooldown~$1.6–2.5M gross

A good two-player choice when you both want one longer planned run. First completion each week pays the bonus shown; repeat runs the same week pay reduced — better to rotate to a different bonus heist than repeat this one.

Doomsday

No cooldown~$650k–1.5M gross

Keep it for a committed session — best when both of you are ready for the full chain. First completion each week pays the bonus; repeat runs the same week pay less, so switching to a different heist for its own bonus is usually the better move.

Cluckin' Bell Farm Raid

No cooldown$500k leader

The quick crew option when you want a proper heist-style run without a long setup. Biggest payout on the week's first run; still doable the same day after that, just for less — a different heist is better value on a repeat.

Kortz Center

~48 min cooldown~$1.9–2.2M first run

Unlike the other three, this one has a real cooldown — about 48 minutes with a two-to-four player crew before you can replay it, longer solo. First completion each week is the big one; a replay after the cooldown pays a reduced cut. Its rotating reward can push the take higher some weeks — check the weekly rewards below before deciding whether to prioritise it.
